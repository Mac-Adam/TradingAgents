## Important Notes About ETF Structure

Since **TEST** is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund), it differs significantly from traditional equity companies:

### ETF Characteristics
1. **No Traditional Balance Sheet**: ETFs don't have operations in the traditional sense; instead, they hold a basket of underlying assets
2. **Income Distribution Focus**: Yields on ETFs come from dividends of underlying holdings or options expiration (in the case of YieldMax products)
3. **Expense Ratio**: Costs are typically reflected through ongoing management fees and the distribution of the underlying ETF
4. **Transparency**: Most ETFs are held daily for the underlying securities with a 4-5 day lag in NAV updates

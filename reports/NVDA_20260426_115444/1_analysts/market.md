### 1. Trend Analysis: Moving Averages (SMA/EMA)

Moving averages smooth out price fluctuations to determine the underlying trend.

*   **Observation:** The price action has consistently traded above shorter-term averages (like the 20-day SMA) and is holding above major intermediate averages (like the 50-day SMA).
*   **Interpretation:** This confirms a **strong long-term uptrend**. Any sustained drop below the 50-day SMA would signal a significant trend reversal, but current support levels suggest the uptrend remains intact.
*   **Support Levels:** Key support is found near the **\$190 - \$200** range, which has successfully held during minor pullbacks.

### 2. Momentum Analysis: Relative Strength Index (RSI)

The RSI measures the speed and change of price movements. (Typically, readings above 70 suggest overbought, and below 30 suggest oversold.)

*   **Current Reading:** The RSI has recently seen fluctuations. While it has dipped into neutral territory, it is currently recovering support.
*   **Interpretation:** When the RSI cools off (moves out of extreme overbought territory, e.g., dropping from 80 to 65), it suggests that the recent rapid run-up might have paused, giving investors room to breathe without confirming a trend reversal.
*   **Caution:** Extreme overbought readings often precede a minor retracement to establish a new support baseline.

### 3. Direction & Strength Analysis: MACD

The MACD indicator measures the relationship between two exponential moving averages to confirm momentum streaks (crossovers).

*   **Observation:** We are looking for the MACD line to remain above the center line (zero) and for the signal line to *continue* making higher highs.
*   **Interpretation:** The sustained positive divergence (Divergence means the price might dip, but the MACD histogram predicts the next bounce) confirms that the **primary bullish momentum is still in place.** Bullish crossovers are favored over bearish crossovers right now.

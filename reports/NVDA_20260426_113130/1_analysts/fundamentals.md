### **NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) Income Statement Analysis (2022–2026)**  
**Key Financial Trends and Insights**  

---

#### **1. Revenue & Profitability Growth**  
- **Total Revenue**:  
  - **2022**: $26.97B → **2026**: $215.94B (a **680% leap**)  
  - Driven by AI demand, GPU adoption, and data center growth.  
- **Net Income**:  
  - **2022**: $4.37B → **2026**: $120.07B (a **2,650% increase**)  
  - Reflects improved margins and operational efficiency.  

- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**:  
  - **2022**: $0.174 → **2026**: $4.90 (up **2,700%**).  
  - Diluted EPS growth signals strong shareholder value creation.  

---

#### **2. Cost Structure & Margins**

  - High R&D investment aligns with NVIDIA's focus on AI, HPC, and semiconductors.  

---

#### **3. Tax Efficiency**

  - Lower tax burden amplified net income growth.  
- **Tax Provision**:  
  - **2022**: -$0.19B (tax loss) → **2026**: $21.38B (positive tax contribution).  

---

#### **4. Operational Efficiency**  
- **EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, & Amortization)**:  
  - **2022**: $33.82B → **2026**: $141.45B (a **317% increase**).

- **Operating Income**:

---

#### **5. Key Risks & Considerations**  
- **One-Time Charges**:

  - May impact short-term results but does not detract from long-term growth.  
- **Share Dilution**:  
  - Diluted shares increased from ~24.87B (2022) to ~24.90B (2026), slightly diluting EPS but minimal impact.
